Cantor Scheduler

Cantors sign up for Masses from a personal link: no accounts, no passwords. The director sees the whole schedule, fills gaps, notes each week's music, and emails cantors from the same page.

It's free and runs entirely in your own Google account: the schedule lives in a Google Sheet you own, and emails go out from your Gmail. Nobody else, including whoever made this, can see your Sheet.

Got here from a cantor link that didn't work? Ask your choir director for a new one.

Set it up for your parish

About 10 minutes, all in your web browser. Use a computer, not a phone.

  1. Sign in to Google with the account that should own the schedule. Emails to cantors come from this account, so a parish or music-ministry account is a good choice.
  2. Make your copy.
    Copy the template Sheet
    Then click Make a copy. Keep the copy's name or rename it; either is fine.
  3. Turn it on. In your copy, choose Extensions ▸ Apps Script. In the window that opens, click Deploy ▸ New deployment. Click the gear next to "Select type" and choose Web app. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone, then click Deploy.
  4. Approve it. Google asks you to authorize access. Click Authorize access and pick your account.
    Expect a security warning. Google will say "Google hasn't verified this app." That's normal for a script you run yourself. Click Advanced, then Go to Cantor Scheduler (unsafe), then Allow.
    It asks to: see and edit this one Sheet; send email as you (the sign-up emails); run on a timer (the optional digest); and see your email address (to fill in Settings). It can't see your other files.
  5. Copy the Web app URL that Google shows when it's done (it ends in /exec), then click Done.
  6. Get your director link. Go back to your Sheet and reload it. A Cantors menu appears (it can take a few seconds). Choose Cantors ▸ Director link, paste the URL from step 5, and open the link it gives you. Bookmark it and keep it private: it's how you manage the schedule.
  7. Set up your parish. The director page opens on Settings: enter your parish's name, Mass times, and time zone. Then add your cantors (Cantor list) and send them their links (Sign-up links).

Getting updates

Your director page says when there's a new version. To update, in your Sheet choose Extensions ▸ Apps Script. On the left under Libraries, click CantorScheduler, pick the highest version, and click Save. Then Deploy ▸ Manage deployments, click the pencil, set Version: New version, and click Deploy. Your links stay the same. If Google asks you to approve it again, it's the same as when you set it up (step 4).
